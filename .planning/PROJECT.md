# Meeeku - Shopify Liquid Theme Project Context

## Project Overview
- **Brand Name**: Meeeku
- **Target Audience**: Kids (Girls 2-5 Years active now; Boys clothing coming soon)
- **Product Categories**:
  1. Indian Ethnic (Festive Lehengas, Anarkali Sets, Kurta Sets for girls 2-5Y)
  2. Nightsuits (Organic Cotton Pyjamas, Soft Printed Sleepwear)
- **Design Aesthetic**: Playful & Pastel Dream
  - Primary colors: Soft Pastel Pink (`#FCE4EC`, `#F8BBD0`), Lavender Purple (`#6B3FA0`), Warm Cream (`#FFFDF7`), Coral/Gold Accents (`#FF80AB`, `#E6C200`)
  - Typography: Soft, rounded, legible modern fonts with playful headings
  - Logo Context: Baby elephant with floral crown on soft pastel scalloped cloud badge
- **GitHub Repository**: `https://github.com/aaybwork-studio/meeeku`
- **Base Shopify Theme**: Official Shopify Dawn (OS 2.0 Standard)
- **Reference Websites**:
  - `https://www.napstory.in/` (Cozy nightwear layout, soft aesthetic)
  - `https://junior.chapter2drip.com/` (Modern kidswear grid & interactive storytelling)

## Key Custom Features & Extensions
1. **Category & Filter Architecture**: Category-first navigation (Indian Ethnic, Nightsuits) with Age (2-5Y) and Gender filters, plus a "Boys Collection - Launching Soon" dedicated section.
2. **Custom Liquid Sections & Highlights**:
   - Hero Banners with tabbed dynamic sliders for Indian Ethnic vs Nightsuits.
   - "Boys Collection - Launching Soon" section with email waitlist modal & teaser cards.
   - Kids Interactive Size & Fit Guide (2Y, 3Y, 4Y, 5Y measurements for Ethnic & Nightwear).
3. **Shopping UX**: Sticky Quick-Add / Express Checkout Drawer, Product Badging ("Soft 100% Cotton", "Festive Ready", "Handcrafted").
4. **Placeholder Assets**: High-definition AI placeholder images for products & collection banners generated via Nanobana.
